# Design <System Name>

1. **Clarify requirements**: functional and non-functional, scope, out-of-scope
2. **Estimate scale**: users, QPS, storage, bandwidth
3. **Define APIs**: endpoints, request/response, protocols
4. **Data model**: entities, SQL vs NoSQL, indexing
5. **High-level design**: components and data flow (diagram)
6. **Deep dives**: the 2-3 hardest components
7. **Scale and reliability**: caching, sharding, replication, queues, failure modes
8. **Trade-offs**: alternatives considered and why they were rejected
9. **Wrap-up**: monitoring, security, future improvements
